## 2. Logic Chain

1. **Observation 1 & 2** show that the middleware route guard for `/admin` and the server-side page guard in `app/admin/page.tsx` are explicitly commented out.
   * *Reasoning:* Any unauthenticated visitor accessing `/admin` will gain full administrative visibility over orders, customer details, products, and abandoned carts.
   * *Step Conclusion:* This constitutes a **CRITICAL** security vulnerability that blocks production launch.

2. **Observation 3** shows that `.env.local` stores high-privilege keys, including database credentials and `SUPABASE_SERVICE_ROLE_KEY`, while `STRIPE_WEBHOOK_SECRET` remains unconfigured.
   * *Reasoning:* Unset webhook secrets allow spoofed payment events, and lack of `.env.example` increases deployment error risks.
   * *Step Conclusion:* Credential rotation and `.env.example` setup are mandatory for **R4 Security**.

3. **Observation 4 & 5** demonstrate the complete absence of statutory company details in `Footer.tsx` and missing policy pages (`/privacy`, `/cookie-policy`, `/condizioni-vendita`).
   * *Reasoning:* Italian Civil Code (Art. 2250) and GDPR require e-commerce sites to display P.IVA, REA, PEC, and provide explicit Privacy, Cookie, and Terms policies before processing transactions.
   * *Step Conclusion:* Non-compliance creates legal liability (**R5 GDPR & Legal**).

4. **Observation 6 & 7** confirm that `app/layout.tsx` retains default Next.js boilerplate title/description, lacks `sitemap.xml` / `robots.txt`, lacks analytics tags (GTM, Meta Pixel, GA4, CAPI), and uses unoptimized `<img>` tags on `app/page.tsx`.
   * *Reasoning:* Search engines will index placeholder metadata, and marketing tracking cannot record conversions or user metrics. Unoptimized images increase LCP / page loading times.
   * *Step Conclusion:* Full remediation of metadata, analytics, sitemaps, and images is required for **R6 SEO, Analytics & Performance**.
